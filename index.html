Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

MSJapan Research Tool is an internal tool used for managing eBay-related inventory, listings, and seller account information.

Information We Access

When authorized by the user through eBay OAuth, the tool may access information necessary to provide its functions, including eBay account information, inventory information, listing information, business policies, inventory locations, and other seller-related data authorized by the user.

How We Use Information

Information obtained from eBay is used only for operating and managing MSJapan Research Tool and related eBay selling activities.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell eBay user data. We do not disclose eBay user data to third parties except when required to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or as otherwise authorized by the user.

Authentication Information

OAuth credentials and tokens are treated as confidential information. Reasonable technical measures are used to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or use.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the operation of the tool, record keeping, security, and compliance purposes.

User Authorization

Access to eBay account information is granted through eBay OAuth. Users may revoke authorization through their eBay account settings where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality or data handling practices of MSJapan Research Tool change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding MSJapan Research Tool, please contact the operator of the tool.
